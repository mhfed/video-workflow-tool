## Video direction

Palette system: Broadside two-register system only — ink-black ground with cream text and fire-orange emphasis; orange declaration frames use ink-black text. Barlow display is the main graphic primitive; IBM Plex Mono is limited to tiny labels and day counters.

Motion grammar: Every reveal follows the spoken cue, using smooth long-tail settles. Kinetic phrases arrive as hard-cut swaps, masked rises, or one decisive beat-slam; diagrams build one part at a time. No element dump in the first quarter of a frame. Holds are genuinely still after the final cue, with no breathing loop or late camera drift.

Rhythm: Frames 1–2 are fast and confrontational; Frame 3 deliberately holds the diagnosis for a short breath; Frame 4 becomes measured and procedural; Frame 5 returns to a decisive poster cadence and holds the final question.

Negative list: no gradients, shadows, rounded cards, stock imagery, browser chrome, decorative bokeh, bouncy default entrances, screensaver motion, or slideshow-style front-loading. Keep all load-bearing content above the bottom caption keep-out band.
