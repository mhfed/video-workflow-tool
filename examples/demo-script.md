# Why Small Habits Beat Motivation

Motivation feels powerful, but it is unreliable. Some days we wake up ready to work, and other days even a small task feels heavy.

Small habits solve a different problem. Instead of asking for a burst of energy, they reduce the amount of energy needed to begin.

Imagine wanting to read more. A goal like reading fifty pages every night sounds impressive, but it creates friction. Reading two pages before bed is easier to start, and starting is the part that matters most.

Once an action becomes familiar, the brain spends less effort deciding whether to do it. The routine begins to run with less negotiation.

That is why consistency often beats intensity. A tiny action repeated for months can travel farther than a heroic effort repeated only a few times.
